Our Partners

The missions we're
raising for right now.

Project 22 is currently engaged with three Tampa Bay–area organizations, raising funds to fuel their frontline work. Every bundle sold, every duck scanned, every sponsor secured flows toward these missions.

Ruskin, Florida · Est. 40+ years

Mary & Martha House

For over four decades, Mary & Martha House has offered a sanctuary of safety and support to women and their children who have experienced the profound challenges of domestic violence and homelessness in South Hillsborough County.

  • Two residential shelter facilities where families have space of their own.
  • Individualized case management for physical and emotional needs.
  • Employment-readiness training — with 94% of program completers achieving permanent employment and housing.
Visit marymarthahouse.org
Lithia, Florida · Non-denominational

Appointed Church

A church family anchored in John 15:16 — "You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you that you should go and bear fruit." Appointed Church serves the Lithia and FishHawk communities through worship, discipleship, and hands-on outreach.

  • Sunday worship, home groups, and prayer ministry.
  • Children's and student programs that ignite the next generation.
  • Community events like the Back2School Bash and Harvest Hoedown.
Visit appointed.church
Gibsonton, Florida · 501(c)(3)

Know Us By Our Love

Bikers, believers, and bridge-builders. Know Us By Our Love walks the Freedom Road with men whose stories started in hard places — behind bars, inside addiction, or in homes that broke before they did. Their conviction: your past doesn't disqualify you. It prepares you.

  • Trauma-informed mental health counseling and recovery services.
  • Sober living, skill-building, and spiritual restoration.
  • Prison and facility ministry outreach across Tampa Bay.
Visit knowusbyourlove.org
"By this everyone will know that you are my disciples, if you love one another." — John 13:35

Know Us By Our Love, Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization in Gibsonton, Florida. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
